# 7. Six-month content strategy (October 2026 – March 2027)

## 7.1 Principles

- **Every article must lead to listings.** A piece earns a slot only if it can link to a hub page and at least three place pages by name. Generic pet-care topics (the four existing care posts) stay online, get internal links to the directory, but are not expanded: they attract readers who are not looking for places and they compete with vet and pet-shop blogs.
- **Data-backed lists beat opinion lists.** "20 pet friendly kafića u Beogradu" is pulled from the directory (verified date, dog size, indoor rule shown), so the article stays true as the data changes and can be refreshed by regenerating the list.
- **Seasonality drives the calendar:** autumn hikes (Oct), Kopaonik and New Year (Nov–Dec), winter travel and documents (Jan), summer-at-sea planning starts in February for Serbian families, spring trips and Prvi maj in March.
- **Cadence:** 9–10 articles per month (about two per week) is the plan below; if capacity is lower, keep the P1-tagged items and push P2 items a month.
- **Format standards:** 900–1,800 words; one H1; H2 per place/section; a "Brzi odgovor" box at the top; photos with alt text; author with name and bio; "Ažurirano" date; a CTA block after the first third and at the end; 3–5 FAQ questions; BlogPosting schema; internal links per section 8.
- **Refresh policy:** seasonal pieces get a yearly refresh (same URL, updated title year); listicles are regenerated when the underlying places change.

## 7.4 Production notes

- Every list article is generated from a saved directory query (city + category + sort by verified date) so it can be regenerated in one click; editors add the narrative around it.
- Each destination guide is written after a real visit, or with a named local contributor; photos of the author's dog on location are the cheapest trust signal available.
- Articles get an author page (`/autor/{slug}`) with bio and social links; this supports E-E-A-T and gives journalists a person to quote.
- Track per article in GA4: clicks to hub pages, clicks to place pages, "add a place" starts. Articles that do not produce directory clicks within 60 days get their CTAs and links reworked before any new article is written.
